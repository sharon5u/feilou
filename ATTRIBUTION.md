# Map attribution

Country and territory outlines derived from Natural Earth through the datasets/geo-countries collection:
https://github.com/datasets/geo-countries
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Natural Earth map data is public domain. This bundled derivative rounds coordinates and reduces point counts for the first-draft map. Boundary depictions are illustrative and do not establish political status or legal boundaries. Country centers are approximations for navigating the map.

## Current interactive map

Leaflet 1.9.4: https://leafletjs.com/ — BSD-2-Clause; license bundled in dist/vendor/leaflet/LICENSE.
Map tiles and geocoding data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
Search service: Nominatim https://nominatim.org/
The earlier Natural Earth outline asset is retained but no longer displayed.

`dist/assets/world-style.json` is a simplified decorative derivative of the bundled `world.json`, using the same source attribution as that file. Region colors are an illustrative design layer.
